🏗️ Construction Operations & Digital FTE Roster

How Fynsera AI agents run coordination work across office, job site, yard, and fabrication plant operations.

🏢 Office & Management Operations

  • Project Intelligence Agent: Replaces status calls; generates live project health dashboards from real site data.
  • Field Voice Agent: Converts superintendent voice notes & WhatsApp audio into structured daily logs.
  • Scheduling Agent: Automatically re-syncs Primavera schedules as site inputs update.
  • Procurement Agent: Manages supplier RFQs, quote comparisons, and delivery tracking.
  • Cost Intelligence Agent: Monitors invoice line items against contract budgets to catch margin risk.
  • Contract & Compliance Agent: Flags scope creep and contract compliance risks before claims arise.

🚜 Yard, Plant & Site Operations

  • Prefab Fabrication Agent: Tracks off-site component fabrication against installation schedules.
  • BIM-to-Fabrication Agent: Converts 3D model changes into updated shop tickets and cut lists.
  • Quality Inspection Agent: Verifies manufactured components against specs using computer vision & photos.
  • Inventory & Yard Agent: Tracks warehouse & yard stock levels in real time to prevent shortages.
  • Equipment & Fleet Agent: Monitors machinery maintenance, fuel, and idle time across jobsites.
  • Punch List & Closeout Agent: Accelerates final inspections, warranty docs, and project closeout.

🛡️ The Governance System (Workforce OS)

Level 1–4 Autonomy

No agent gets a blank check. Agents start at L1 (human reviews all actions) and earn higher autonomy only on verified results.

Hard Exit Safeguards

Unusual clauses, legal ambiguities, or cost approvals exceeding preset budget thresholds automatically defer to human decision-makers.

Complete Audit Ledger

Every input, decision, risk flag, and human approval is logged permanently. Zero black box, total accountability.
