# Pricing & Plans

## The 60-Day Pilot Program

Every new Fynsera relationship starts with a 60-day pilot on a single live project — not a company-wide contract. The pilot is intentionally structured to be low-risk and self-evidencing:

- Scoped to **one project**, measured against that project's own numbers, not industry averages
- Typically begins with the **Project Intelligence Agent** and **Field Voice Agent**, since they show measurable time savings fastest
- Includes a defined stop point: if the pilot isn't showing measurable value early, the engagement ends there — the risk is bounded to the one project, not the business

The pilot exists to answer one question with real data before any larger commitment is made: does this actually save time and money on a project you can verify yourself.

## Ongoing Plans After the Pilot

If a pilot proves out, ongoing plans scale with usage rather than requiring a large upfront license:

- **Per-project, per-agent structure** — cost scales with how many projects and which agents are active, not a flat company-wide fee regardless of use
- **No penalty for scaling slowly** — a company can add one project at a time, at its own pace, without a contractual obligation to scale faster than it's ready for
- **Phase 2 (yard & plant agents)** are typically priced and rolled out separately, once the office roster is stable — see [Implementation & Rollout](./08-implementation-rollout.md)

The guiding principle behind pricing is the same as the guiding principle behind the rollout itself: cost should always track proven value, not a projection of it.

## Cost FAQ

**Does the pilot cost anything?**
The 60-day pilot has an associated cost, scoped to the one project it runs on — significantly smaller than a company-wide commitment, and structured so that the ROI it's expected to surface (see [Implementation & Rollout](./08-implementation-rollout.md#reading-your-roi-dashboard)) should outweigh it well within the pilot window itself.

**What happens if the pilot doesn't show ROI?**
The engagement stops at the defined checkpoint. There's no further obligation — the exposure was limited to the one pilot project from the start.

**How is pricing structured once we scale to more projects?**
Ongoing pricing is usage-based — tied to the number of active projects and agents — rather than a single company-wide license fee. This keeps cost proportional to value at every stage of a rollout.

**Is there a cost difference between office agents and yard/plant agents?**
Yes — Phase 2 agents are scoped and priced separately, since they typically come online after the office roster has already proven itself, on a different timeline and with different integration requirements (shop systems, BIM tools, inventory systems).
