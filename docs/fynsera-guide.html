Fynsera — Quick Guide

A short guide to what Fynsera is, how it works, and how to get started. For the full in-depth reference, see the complete Fynsera book.

1. Welcome to Fynsera​

Fynsera is an AI workforce built for construction companies. It runs on two systems working together:

  • The Nervous System — senses what's happening on every site in real time: schedules, field progress, costs, contracts, and approvals, all in one place.
  • The Governance System — makes sure nothing happens without your rules and your sign-off. Every action is logged, reviewed, and accountable to a named person.

Together, these two systems let AI agents handle the coordination work that currently eats hours out of every project — status calls, retyped updates, chasing suppliers, searching contracts — while your people keep the judgment, relationships, and craft that only humans can own.

2. Core Concepts​

The System of Record
Today, the truth about a project is scattered — a schedule in Primavera, progress in a superintendent's head, costs in spreadsheets, contracts in a shared drive, approvals in email threads. The System of Record is the one place everyone, and every agent, trusts as the current, correct answer. Nothing in Fynsera works without this foundation being solid first.

The Nervous System (Real-Time by Design)
Every agent runs its own "heartbeat" — it wakes on a schedule or a live event, checks the System of Record, decides, and acts, 24 hours a day. Nobody has to open an app or remember to trigger it.

The Governance Layer (Workforce OS)
Every agent — a "Digital FTE" — reads and writes through one governing layer, never directly into your systems. This layer runs five functions:

FunctionWhat it does
HireBring a new Digital FTE onto the roster for a defined task
AssignRoute work to the right agent, with the right scope
GovernEnforce budget limits, approval rules, and what each agent may touch
ObserveKeep a full ledger of who did what, at what cost, with what result
RetireRemove an agent or role the moment it's no longer needed

Autonomy Levels
No agent gets a blank check. Every agent starts supervised and earns more independence over time:

LevelWhat the agent doesWhere the human is
L1Drafts or acts; human reviews everythingReviews all output
L2Acts; a verifier checks; human reviews exceptions onlyReviews exceptions
L3Acts within set limits; batches escalationsReviews batched escalations
L4Runs the task type independently, within approved scopeReviews a weekly report

An agent always has a hard exit: the moment it hits a legal question, an unusual clause, or a cost above a set limit, it stops and names a human to decide.

How Data Flows
Field → System of Record → Agents → Human Review → Action
Data comes in from the site (voice, photo, WhatsApp), lands in the shared record, agents act on it, only exceptions reach a person, and approved actions go out automatically. Every step is logged — nothing is a black box.

4. Governance & Control​

  • Autonomy is earned, not granted. An agent moves up a level only after repeated, verified success on that exact task.
  • Every approval above L1 has a name on it. Accountability never disappears into "the system."
  • The audit log is complete. What data came in, which agent acted, what it decided, and who approved it — always visible, never hidden.
  • Hard exits are non-negotiable. Legal risk, unusual clauses, or costs above a limit always stop the agent and route to a human.

5. Security & Data​

Your contracts, financials, and project data stay inside your own systems of record. Agents connect to your data — they do not remove it, copy it elsewhere, or operate outside the scope you define. Access is limited to what each agent needs for its specific task.

6. Implementation & Rollout​

Fynsera starts on one live project, not a company-wide rollout — this is intentional, since most failed AI pilots trace back to messy data, not bad technology.

7. Pricing & Plans​

Fynsera starts with a 60-day pilot on one project, measured against your own numbers. If it doesn't deliver measurable savings early, the engagement stops — you've risked one project, not the business. Ongoing plans after the pilot scale with the number of projects and agents in use, not a large upfront license.

8. FAQs​

Will this replace my people?
No. Agents take the retyping, chasing, and paperwork. People keep final accountability, client relationships, judgment on the unusual, safety leadership, and training the next generation.

Who's liable if an agent is wrong?
Every action is evidence-linked and logged. Nothing acts above L1 until it has earned trust, and any high-risk action always stops for human sign-off.

Is our data safe?
Yes — your systems of record stay yours. Agents connect to them; they don't leave them.

We tried AI before and got no ROI — why would this be different?
Most failed pilots trace to messy data and company-wide rollouts done too fast. Fynsera starts on one clean project specifically to avoid that failure mode.

9. Glossary​

  • Digital FTE — An AI agent treated as a roster member: hired, assigned, governed, observed, and retired like a role, not just a script.
  • System of Record — The single trusted source of current, correct project data that every person and agent reads and writes to.
  • Nervous System — The real-time sensing layer that lets agents wake, check, decide, and act continuously.
  • Governance Layer / Workforce OS — The layer that sits between agents and your systems, enforcing rules, budgets, and accountability.
  • Autonomy Level (L1–L4) — The scale describing how much independent action an agent has earned, from full human review to independent operation within scope.
  • Hard Exit — A built-in stopping point where an agent always defers to a human, regardless of its autonomy level.
